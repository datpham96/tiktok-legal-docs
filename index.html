How it works

Four steps, and you stay in control at every one of them.

  1. Connect your social account through the platform's official Login Kit (OAuth 2.0).
  2. AutoPublisher researches a topic and plans a multi-scene storyboard for your video.
  3. AI generates scene visuals, captions, and hashtags based on your selected topic.
  4. Review everything in Studio, choose your privacy settings, then publish.
